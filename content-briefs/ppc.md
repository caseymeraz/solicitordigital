### Irish compliance angle (lead with this, it is the differentiator)
Google Ads for solicitors in the Republic of Ireland must respect the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules and the Solicitors (Advertising) Regulations. Critically, Irish law restricts advertising that solicits or encourages personal injury claims: direct response style personal injury advertising and "no win no fee" style messaging are not permitted. State this plainly and early. The practical line for the page: we will not run banned personal injury direct response ads, and we build every campaign, keyword list, ad and landing page to stay inside the LSRA rules, with GDPR handled correctly on forms and tracking. This is exactly why a generic UK or US agency is the wrong fit for an Irish firm. Do not use the phrase "no win no fee" anywhere on the page except, if needed, to name it as something not permitted.
