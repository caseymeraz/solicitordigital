- **Internal linking** (content into practice-area and location pages)
- **Content audit, refresh and repurposing** (updating dated legal content, keeping it accurate, repurposing across email and social)
- **Measurement** (enquiries influenced by content, rankings, organic traffic, cost per client, not vanity metrics)
