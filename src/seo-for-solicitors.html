The problem

If you are not on page one, you are invisible to most of your future clients

The way people find a solicitor has changed completely. The Golden Pages era is over. Today someone with a legal problem in Dublin, Cork or anywhere in Ireland opens Google, types something like "conveyancing solicitor near me" or "personal injury solicitor Cork", and picks from what they see first.

Around 7 in 10 people turn online at some point before they pick up the phone. The firms that show up in the map pack and the top organic results get the call. The firms on page two rarely get found at all, no matter how good their work is.

SEO is the closest thing to a tap you can turn on. Done well, it brings a steady flow of people who are actively looking for the work you do, at a lower cost per client than ads alone, and it keeps paying back month after month.

What we do

What law firm SEO actually involves

Ranking a solicitor's website is not one thing, it is several working together. Here is the work that gets Irish firms found, and what each part does for you.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Most people looking for a solicitor search locally, and the map pack sits right at the top. We optimise and manage your Google Business Profile, fix your citations and your name, address and phone details across the web, and build a steady stream of genuine reviews so your firm shows up when local clients look.

On page SEO and practice area pages

We build dedicated pages for each practice area and location you want clients for, with the right titles, headings, internal links and genuinely useful content. This is how you rank for terms like "family law solicitor Galway" rather than a single generic homepage that ranks for nothing.

Technical SEO

A fast, secure, mobile first website that Google can crawl and understand. We handle Core Web Vitals and page speed, site structure, indexation, HTTPS and schema markup for legal services, so the foundations never hold your rankings back.

Content and EEAT

Google holds legal content to a high standard because it affects people's lives and money. We create clear, accurate content that demonstrates your experience, expertise, authority and trust, answers the questions clients actually ask, and earns the rankings that thin pages never will.

Link building and citations

Authority is earned through quality links and consistent listings, from relevant Irish legal directories and the Law Society to genuine digital PR. We focus on links that actually move rankings for legal sites, never spam.

Reviews and reputation

Reviews influence both your local rankings and whether a client calls you or the firm next door. We put a simple, compliant system in place that asks happy clients for a review at the right moment and helps you respond well.

Built for Irish firms

SEO that stays inside the LSRA rules

Marketing a law firm in Ireland is not the same as marketing any other business. The Legal Services Regulatory Authority sets clear limits on how solicitors can advertise under the Legal Services Regulation Act 2015, with particular restrictions around personal injury work. Get it wrong and you risk more than a wasted budget.

We know those rules, and we build every page, profile, review request and piece of content to stay the right side of them: truthful, professional and never misleading. We also handle GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 properly, with compliant forms and cookie consent and secure handling of the enquiries your site collects.

Compliance areas we cover

  • LSRA advertising restrictions, including personal injury
  • Truthful, non misleading claims and outcome language
  • Proper disclaimers on solicitor websites
  • GDPR compliant contact forms and data handling
  • Cookie consent that meets Irish standards
  • Review and testimonial compliance
You focus on practising law. We handle the marketing and keep it compliant, end to end.

FAQ

SEO for solicitors, common questions

How long does SEO take to work for a solicitor?▼
Most firms see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, with the compounding gains arriving over 6 to 12 months. Local SEO and Google Business Profile work can show results sooner, often within weeks. SEO is a long game that keeps paying back, so we set honest expectations and report progress every month.
How much does SEO cost for a law firm in Ireland?▼
We price each service individually rather than in fixed packages, and build the plan around your goals and budget. You can see indicative figures on our pricing page. You get a clear quote after your free SEO audit, with no surprises.
Can you guarantee a number one ranking on Google?▼
No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Nobody controls Google's results. What we can do is the proven work that earns rankings over time: a technically sound site, strong local signals, genuine content and authoritative links, all measured against enquiries rather than vanity positions.
Is SEO compliant with LSRA advertising rules?▼
Yes, when it is done properly. We build every page, profile and piece of content to comply with the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules, including the restrictions around personal injury work, and we handle GDPR and data protection correctly. Compliance is part of the process, not an afterthought.
What is local SEO and the Google map pack for solicitors?▼
When someone searches for a solicitor in their area, Google shows a map with three local listings at the top, known as the map pack. Local SEO is the work that gets your firm into it: an optimised Google Business Profile, consistent citations and name, address and phone details, and a steady stream of genuine reviews. For most firms it drives the highest quality enquiries.
Do I need a new website for SEO to work?▼
Not always. We assess what you already have first. Sometimes improving your current site is enough. If it is slow, dated, not mobile friendly or simply not turning visitors into enquiries, a rebuild may deliver a far better return. We give you an honest answer either way.
Do you only work with solicitors in Dublin?▼
No. We work with solicitors across the Republic of Ireland, from Dublin and Cork to Galway, Limerick, Waterford and every county, and we are based in Kinsale, Co. Cork ourselves. Firms outside the capital often see faster results because there is less competition in local search.
How do you measure SEO success for a law firm?▼
We track the things that matter to your practice: keyword rankings, organic traffic, Google Business Profile actions, calls and form enquiries, and ultimately new clients and the cost to win each one. Every month you get a plain English report, not a spreadsheet you need a degree to read.
