The problem

A scattergun approach burns budget and still leaves you behind

Most firms do not have a marketing problem so much as a coordination problem. A bit of budget goes to a website nobody can find, a bit to ads with no tracking, a social account that has gone quiet, and an SEO effort that never really started. Meanwhile the firms that treat this as one connected system pull ahead. Someone with a legal problem in Dublin, Cork or anywhere in Ireland opens Google, searches for the work they need, and picks from the firms that show up first, in the map pack, in the ads and in the organic results. The answer is not more marketing, it is a plan. When your channels point at the same goal and you know what a new client costs to win, every euro works harder and the results compound month after month.

The full picture

The channels we bring together, and the job each one does

Digital marketing for a law firm is not one thing, it is several channels working together, each doing a specific job. Here is what each one does and where it fits, with a link to the detail where there is a dedicated page.

Website design

Your solicitor website is the hub every other channel points to. A fast, mobile first site that earns trust and turns visitors into enquiries is the difference between traffic and actual clients. Everything else is wasted if the page they land on does not convert.

SEO

Law firm SEO is how you get found on Google in the organic results for your practice areas and locations, from "conveyancing solicitor Cork" to "family law solicitor Dublin". It takes time to build, but it brings a steady, lower cost flow of clients who are actively looking for the work you do.

Local SEO and Google Business Profile

Most people looking for a solicitor search locally, and the map pack sits at the very top. Local SEO optimises your Google Business Profile, fixes your citations and name, address and phone details, and builds genuine reviews so you win "solicitor near me" searches.

Google Ads

Google Ads and pay per click put you at the top of the results today, with a cost per enquiry you control. It is the fastest way to test demand and bring clients in while your SEO builds underneath. We run lean, compliant campaigns where every euro is tied back to real enquiries.

Content marketing

Clear, accurate legal content answers the questions your clients are searching for, demonstrates your experience and expertise, and earns the rankings that thin pages never will. It is what builds trust before a client ever calls, and it feeds both SEO and your reputation.

Reviews and reputation

Reviews influence both your local rankings and whether a client calls you or the firm next door. We put a simple, compliant system in place that asks happy clients for a review at the right moment, and helps you respond well to every one.

Two more channels round out the picture. Social media on LinkedIn, Facebook and Instagram keeps your firm visible and builds trust, while email marketing keeps you in touch with past clients and the referrers who send you work. There is also the growing world of AI answer engines and Google AI Overviews, which increasingly sit on these searches, so we build content that earns visibility there too. We fold these in where they fit your goals, rather than for the sake of it.

How the channels fit together

The point is sequencing, not doing everything at once

The mistake most firms make is treating these as a shopping list and trying to switch them all on at the same time. That spreads a finite budget too thin to move the needle anywhere. The channels are far more powerful in the right order, because each one feeds the next.

A solid website has to come first, because it is where every other channel sends people and where enquiries are won or lost. Google Ads can then bring clients in quickly while local SEO and your Google Business Profile build your presence in the map pack. Content and SEO compound underneath over the following months, lowering your cost per client as they grow, and reviews lift both your local rankings and your conversion rate. The job of the plan is to decide what comes first for your firm and what comes later.

Built for Irish firms

Marketing that stays inside the LSRA rules

Marketing a law firm in Ireland is not the same as marketing any other business. The Legal Services Regulatory Authority sets clear limits on how solicitors can advertise under the Legal Services Regulation Act 2015, with particular restrictions around personal injury work. Get it wrong and you risk more than a wasted budget.

We know those rules, and we build every page, profile, ad, review request and piece of content to stay the right side of them: truthful, professional and never misleading. We also handle GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 properly, with compliant forms and cookie consent and secure handling of the enquiries your site collects.

Compliance areas we cover

  • LSRA advertising restrictions, including personal injury
  • Truthful, non misleading claims and outcome language
  • Proper disclaimers on solicitor websites
  • GDPR compliant contact forms and data handling
  • Cookie consent that meets Irish standards
  • Review and testimonial compliance
You focus on practising law. We handle the marketing and keep it compliant, end to end.

FAQ

Digital marketing for solicitors, common questions

What does digital marketing for a solicitor actually include?▼
It is a set of channels working together rather than one thing. The usual mix is a website that turns visitors into enquiries, SEO to get found in organic search, local SEO and your Google Business Profile for the map pack, Google Ads for instant visibility, content that builds trust and rankings, and reviews and reputation. The right combination depends on your goals and budget, which is what the free growth plan works out.
Which channel should my firm start with, SEO or Google Ads?▼
It depends on how quickly you need enquiries and what a case is worth to you. Google Ads brings visibility today and is easy to switch on, so it suits firms that need clients now or want to test demand. SEO and local SEO take longer to build but bring a lower cost per client over time. Most firms do best running ads for the quick wins while SEO compounds underneath, and the growth plan sets the right order for your numbers.
How much should a law firm spend on digital marketing in Ireland?▼
There is no single figure, because it depends on what a case is worth, how many new clients you want and how competitive your area and practice are. We price each service individually rather than in fixed packages and build the plan around your budget. You can see indicative figures on our pricing page, and you get a clear quote after your free growth plan.
How long before digital marketing brings in new clients?▼
It varies by channel. Google Ads can bring enquiries within days of going live. Local SEO and Google Business Profile work often shows in weeks. Organic SEO and content usually take 3 to 6 months to build, with the compounding gains arriving over 6 to 12 months. We set honest expectations up front and report progress every month.
Is digital marketing compliant with the LSRA advertising rules?▼
Yes, when it is done properly. We build every page, profile, ad and piece of content to comply with the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules, including the restrictions around personal injury work, and we handle GDPR and data protection correctly. Compliance is part of the process, not an afterthought.
Do I need to do every channel at once?▼
No, and we would usually advise against it. The point of the plan is to put your budget where it earns the most first, then add channels as the results and the numbers justify it. Many firms start with one or two channels and build from there. You take one piece or the whole engine, and it all works to the same plan.

How do you measure whether the marketing is working?▼
We track the things that matter to your practice: rankings and organic traffic, Google Business Profile actions, ad performance, calls and form enquiries, and ultimately new clients and the cost to win each one.
