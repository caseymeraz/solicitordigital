The problem

If you are not in the map pack, a nearby firm is taking the call

People looking for a solicitor search locally and they search on their phone. Someone in Dublin types "solicitor near me", someone in Cork types "family solicitor Cork", and Google answers with a map and three local firms before a single ordinary result appears. That little map is where the decision gets made.

If your firm is not one of those three, you are relying on the searcher scrolling past the map, past the ads and down into the organic results to find you. Most never do. They tap one of the three listings, read the reviews, and call straight from the screen. The work goes to the firm that showed up, not always the best firm in town.

The map pack is where local clients choose. Get your Google Business Profile and local signals right and you become one of the three firms people actually see and call, at a lower cost per client than ads, every single day.

What we do

What local SEO for solicitors actually involves

Getting a firm into the map pack and keeping it there is not one thing, it is several working together. Here is the work that wins local clients for Irish solicitors, and what each part does for you.

Google Business Profile optimisation

Your Google Business Profile is the listing that appears in the map pack, so it has to be complete and correct. We choose the right primary and secondary categories, fill out your services and description, add photos, post updates, set your attributes and answer the Q and A, and turn on messaging where it suits you. A profile done properly outranks a half filled one every time.

The map pack

The three local listings at the top of the results are ranked on relevance, distance and prominence. We work all three signals so your firm earns a place in the map pack for the searches that matter, like "solicitor near me" and your practice area plus your town, where the highest quality local enquiries live.

NAP citations and consistency

Your name, address and phone details have to match everywhere they appear, from the Law Society of Ireland directory and Golden Pages to Yelp, Hotfrog, Cylex and the main legal directories. We audit every listing, fix the ones that are wrong or out of date, and build the citations that tell Google your firm is a real, settled local business it can trust.

Local keyword research

We map the way local clients actually search: geo qualified terms like "family solicitor Cork", practice area plus city, and the high volume "solicitor near me" intent. We sort them into informational, commercial and ready to call searches, then target the ones that bring you the right clients in the right places.

Reviews and review management

Review volume, how fresh they are and how you respond all feed your local ranking and whether a client calls you or the firm next door. We put a simple, compliant system in place that asks happy clients for a review at the right moment, makes it easy with a direct link, and helps you reply to every one.

Local link building

Local authority is earned through local links: Irish legal directories, local news and media, community sponsorships, and genuine digital PR in the towns you serve. We focus on links that build the prominence Google rewards in local search, never spam.

Location pages

For each office and each town you serve, we build a real location page with content about that area, an embedded map, local title tags and click to call, never thin doorway pages. This is how you rank locally beyond a single homepage and give Google a strong landing page to send map pack clicks to.

Multi office firms

If you have more than one office, each gets its own Google Business Profile, its own local phone number and its own location page. We set it all up so each office ranks in its own area and your offices never compete against each other in search, with NAP details that stay consistent everywhere.

Schema, mobile and measurement

We add LocalBusiness, LegalService, Review and FAQ schema so Google understands your local relevance, keep the site mobile first with fast pages, embedded maps and click to call, and track the numbers that matter: Google Business Profile actions, calls, direction requests and map pack rankings.

Built for Irish firms

Local SEO that stays inside the LSRA rules

Marketing a law firm in Ireland is not the same as marketing any other business. The Legal Services Regulatory Authority sets clear limits on how solicitors can advertise under the Legal Services Regulation Act 2015, with particular restrictions around personal injury work. That applies to your Google Business Profile and your reviews just as much as your website.

Review requests are where firms most often slip up. We use a system that asks happy clients for a review at the right moment, with no incentive, no pressure and nothing misleading, so every review is genuine and compliant. We handle GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 properly too, with compliant forms, secure data handling and the consent the rules require.

Compliance areas we cover

  • LSRA advertising restrictions, including personal injury
  • Compliant review requests, never incentivised or misleading
  • Truthful, non misleading profile and listing content
  • GDPR compliant contact forms and data handling
  • Cookie consent that meets Irish standards
  • Responding to reviews within the rules

What is the Google map pack and why does it matter for solicitors?▼
When someone searches for a solicitor in their area, Google shows a small map with three local listings at the very top, above the normal results. This is the map pack. For solicitors it matters more than almost anything else, because most people searching for legal help nearby pick one of those three firms and call straight from the listing. Getting into the map pack is the single biggest driver of local enquiries, which is why we build the whole strategy around it.
How do you get a law firm into the local map pack?▼
Google ranks the map pack on relevance, distance and prominence. We work all three: a fully optimised Google Business Profile with the right categories and services, consistent name, address and phone details across the web, genuine reviews coming in steadily, local content on your website, and links from Irish sources. There is no single switch, but done together over a few months this is what moves a firm into those three local listings and keeps it there.
