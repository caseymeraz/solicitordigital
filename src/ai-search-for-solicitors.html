FAQ

AI search for solicitors, common questions

What is AI search?▼
AI search is when people get an answer from a tool like ChatGPT, or from the AI answer at the top of Google, instead of a page of blue links. The tool reads the web and gives a short list of firms. Getting your firm onto that list is sometimes called GEO, or answer engine optimisation. We just call it being found in AI.
Will ChatGPT really recommend a solicitor?▼
Yes. We asked ChatGPT for a good personal injury solicitor in Dublin and it named five firms, with their reviews and links. We asked Perplexity for a conveyancing solicitor in Cork and it named five firms and linked to their websites. People ask these tools questions like this every day.
How is this different from SEO?▼
A lot of the work is the same. AI leans on the same things Google does: a clear website, good reviews, and strong listings. So when we improve those, you do better in Google and in AI at the same time. We do them together rather than as two separate jobs.
Is being found in AI allowed under the LSRA rules?▼
Yes. The work is the same kind of marketing as your website and your reviews, and we keep all of it truthful and inside the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules, including the limits on personal injury. We never make false claims to game the AI.
How long does it take to show up in AI?▼
It builds over weeks and months. The AI tools re-read the web all the time, so as your website, reviews and listings improve, your firm starts to appear. The firms that get named tend to have strong reviews and clear sites, and that work takes a little time to add up.
Do I still need normal SEO and a Google Business Profile?▼
Yes, more than ever. AI uses your website, your Google Business Profile, your reviews and your listings to decide who to name. Strong local SEO and AI visibility go hand in hand, so we usually do them together.
Which AI tools do you help with?▼
The main ones people use: ChatGPT, the AI Overviews at the top of Google, Gemini, and Perplexity. The same work helps your firm across all of them, because they read the web in similar ways.
Can you guarantee ChatGPT will name my firm?▼
No, and you should be careful of anyone who says they can. Nobody controls what an AI says. What we can do is the proven work that makes it far more likely your firm gets named, and we check the results so you can see the progress.
