The problem

A firm's website usually ranks for one thing, and it is rarely the work the partners want

Most Irish law firm websites were built as a brochure: a homepage, an about page, a single services page listing everything from conveyancing to commercial litigation, and a team page. Google can only rank a page for what that page is about, so a services page about nine things ranks for none of them.

Meanwhile someone in Limerick types "employment solicitor Limerick" and gets a smaller firm with one clear page on employment law. Your employment department is better. It is also invisible.

Law firm SEO is an architecture problem before it is a content problem. Get the structure right, one page per practice area per office, and every department can win its own search.

What we do

What law firm SEO involves at firm scale

The fundamentals are the same as for any solicitor. The difference is that a firm has to do them for every department and every office at once, in a structure that holds together.

Practice area architecture

One page per practice area, with sub pages for the specific work inside it: not just "family law" but separation, divorce, custody and maintenance. This is the structure that lets a firm rank for hundreds of searches instead of one, and it is the part most firm websites are missing.

Local SEO for every office

A Google Business Profile, a location page and consistent citations for each office, so the Cork office ranks in Cork and the Dublin office ranks in Dublin. We keep the offices from cannibalising each other, which is the most common fault in multi office local SEO.

Solicitor profiles and EEAT

Google wants evidence that real, qualified people stand behind legal content. Solicitor profile pages with qualifications, admissions, practice areas and authorship links to the articles each solicitor writes turn your team page into one of the strongest trust signals on the site.

Technical SEO on a bigger site

A site with a hundred pages has problems a ten page site never sees: crawl waste, duplicate pages, orphaned content, slow templates. We handle Core Web Vitals, site structure, indexation and schema for legal services so the foundations never hold a department back.

Content by department

A publishing plan that gives each practice area the guides, answers and updates its clients search for, written to a legal standard and reviewed by the solicitor whose name goes on it. See how we approach legal content.

Authority and links

Larger firms have assets smaller ones do not: partners who speak at events, published commentary, directory listings, Law Society involvement, press coverage. We turn those into links and mentions that move rankings, and we never buy spam.

SEO that stays inside the LSRA rules, across every department

The Legal Services Regulatory Authority sets clear limits on how solicitors can advertise under the Legal Services Regulation Act 2015, with particular restrictions around personal injury work. In a larger firm the risk is not that one person does not know the rules. It is that content goes out from several departments and nobody checks it against them.

We build a review step into the process so every page, profile, review request and article is checked before it goes live: truthful, professional and never misleading. FAQ

Law firm SEO, common questions

Is SEO for lawyers the same as SEO for solicitors in Ireland?▼
In Ireland, yes. Clients search for a solicitor, Google treats lawyer and solicitor as near synonyms, and the work involved is identical. The difference is scale. A sole practitioner needs a handful of pages ranking in one town. A firm with several partners, practice areas and offices needs an architecture where every department and every office can rank without competing against each other. This page is about that second job.
How much does law firm SEO cost in Ireland?▼
It depends on how many practice areas and offices you want to rank and how competitive each is. We price each service individually and build the plan around your goals and budget, so a two office firm with three departments pays for a different scope than a single office practice. Indicative figures are on our pricing page and you get a clear quote after your free SEO audit.
How long does SEO take to work for a law firm?▼
Most firms see meaningful movement in 3 to 6 months, with the compounding gains arriving over 6 to 12 months. Local SEO and Google Business Profile work for individual offices can show results sooner, often within weeks. Larger firms usually see the first wins in the departments with the least competition, which then fund the harder ones.
We have offices in more than one town. How does SEO handle that?▼
Each office gets its own Google Business Profile, its own location page on the website, and its own local citations, so Cork ranks in Cork and Galway ranks in Galway. Practice area pages then link to the relevant office pages. Done wrong, offices cannibalise each other and only one ranks. Done right, every office competes in its own local search on its own merits.
Do individual solicitors in the firm need their own pages?▼
Yes. Google holds legal content to a high standard because it affects people's lives and money, and it looks for evidence of real experience and expertise. Solicitor profile pages with qualifications, practice areas, notable work and a proper author link to the content each solicitor writes are one of the strongest trust signals a law firm website can carry. They also rank for the solicitor's own name, which is how referrals check you out.
We already have a marketing manager. How do you work with them?▼
Very well, and it is usually the best setup. Your marketing manager owns the relationship with the partners and knows what work the firm wants. We bring the legal search specialism, the technical work and the content production, and we report in a format they can take straight into a partners' meeting. Many of our relationships in the US run exactly this way.
Can you guarantee a number one ranking on Google?▼
No, and you should be wary of anyone who does. Nobody controls Google's results. What we can do is the proven work that earns rankings over time: a technically sound site, a clear practice area and office architecture, strong local signals, genuine content and authoritative links, all measured against enquiries rather than vanity positions.
Is law firm SEO compliant with LSRA advertising rules?▼
Yes, when it is done properly. We build every page, profile and piece of content to comply with the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules, including the restrictions around personal injury work, and we handle GDPR and data protection correctly. For a larger firm that means a review process before anything goes live, so no department publishes something the firm would rather it had not.
