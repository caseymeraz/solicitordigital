About

A US legal marketing agency's playbook, run from Kinsale

Solicitor Digital is the Irish arm of Juris Digital, and it is run by the person who founded both.

Casey Meraz

I founded Juris Digital, a digital marketing agency in the United States built exclusively for law firms. Over more than 15 years it has worked with 250 plus firms, from sole practitioners to multi office practices, on websites, SEO and paid search in the most competitive legal market there is.

I have lived in Kinsale, Co. Cork for over six years. Solicitor Digital exists because the work we do for American firms is years ahead of what most Irish solicitors have been offered, and because I wanted to do it here, for firms I can drive to, inside the rules Irish solicitors actually work under.

What Solicitor Digital is

A marketing partner for solicitors and law firms in the Republic of Ireland, and nothing else. We do not take on accountants, dentists or hotels. That focus is the reason we know the Law Society, the Legal Services Regulatory Authority advertising rules and the personal injury restrictions well enough to build marketing that stays inside them, and the reason we know which cases are worth chasing and which are not.

The services are the ones a firm needs and no more: SEO, local SEO and Google Business Profile, Google Ads, website design, content and AI search visibility, with dedicated pages for larger firms on law firm SEO, law firm website design and working with us as a law firm marketing agency. Every price is on the pricing page.

How I work

Mostly async, by email. Solicitors bill their time and so do I, so I would rather send you a clear written plan you can read at ten at night than book a call to say the same thing. When a decision needs a conversation, we have the conversation.

Every engagement starts with a free audit or review of your firm's current position, which you get whether or not you go on to work with us. Plans are built around what a case is worth to your firm and what you can sensibly spend to win one. There is no long term contract after the initial build. The monthly report is what keeps the relationship going.

The company

Solicitor Digital is a trading name of Ethical Digital Marketing Limited, an Irish company based at Unit 2 Pier Rd, Dromderrig, Kinsale, Co. Cork, P17 V124. Ethical Digital Marketing also works with Cork and Kinsale businesses outside the legal sector under its own name.
