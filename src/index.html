Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what solicitors ask us most.

How does your pricing work? ▼
We do not sell fixed packages. We start from your goals and what a new case is worth to you, then reverse-engineer a plan that fits your budget. Most ongoing engagements start from around EUR 1,500 per month and scale with how competitive your market is and how fast you want to grow. You get a clear quote after your free growth plan, with no surprises.
Do you handle everything, or just SEO? ▼
Everything. SEO, Google Ads, website design and content marketing, run as one joined-up plan. You can take a single piece or the whole engine. It all works towards the same goal: more of the right clients for your firm.
How quickly will I see results? ▼
Google Ads can bring enquiries within days. SEO and content build over time, with meaningful movement typically in 3 to 6 months and the compounding gains coming over 6 to 12. We often combine both so you get near-term leads while the long-term engine builds.
What does reverse-engineering from my numbers actually mean? ▼
If you know what a new client or matter is worth and roughly what you can invest, we work backwards: how many enquiries you need, which channels deliver them most cost-effectively, and what plan fits your budget. If you do not know your numbers yet, we help you work them out first.
Is this compliant with LSRA advertising rules? ▼
Yes. We build every page, campaign and ad to comply with the Legal Services Regulatory Authority rules, including the restrictions around personal injury work, and we handle GDPR and data protection properly. Compliance is built into the process, not bolted on afterwards.
Do I need a new website? ▼
Not always. We assess what you already have first. Sometimes improving your current site is enough. If it is slow, dated or simply not turning visitors into enquiries, a rebuild may deliver a far better return. We give you an honest answer either way.
Do you only work with firms in Dublin? ▼
No. We work with solicitors across Ireland, from Cork and Galway to Limerick, Waterford and every county, and we are based in Kinsale, Co. Cork ourselves. Firms outside Dublin often see faster results because there is less competition in local search.
Do you lock me into a long contract? ▼
No long-term lock-in. We do suggest giving the plan a few months to work, because SEO and content take time to compound, but we earn the next month with results rather than contract terms.
