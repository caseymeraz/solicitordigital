What we build

What a solicitor website should actually do

A good looking site is not enough. A law firm website has a job: load fast, build trust and guide a worried visitor from their first question to contacting you, all while staying compliant. Here is what we build into every one.

Mobile first responsive design

Most people looking for a solicitor are on a phone, often in a stressful moment. We design mobile first, so the layout, type and buttons work perfectly on a small screen before scaling up to tablet and desktop. No pinching, no zooming, no broken layouts on any device.

Page speed and Core Web Vitals

Slow sites lose visitors and rank worse. We build lightweight, optimised pages and tune them against Core Web Vitals, so your site loads quickly even on a mobile connection. Fast pages keep people reading and give Google one more reason to rank you well.

Conversion focused layout

Every page is built to move a visitor towards making contact. We use clear wording that talks about the client's problem rather than just listing your credentials, with calls to action, your phone number and an enquiry form always within easy reach. Design with a purpose, not decoration.

Practice area page structure

We build a dedicated page for each area of law and each location you want clients for, rather than cramming everything onto one generic homepage. This is how you rank for terms like "conveyancing solicitor Cork" and answer the specific questions a client in that situation is actually asking.

Click to call and contact forms

Making contact has to be effortless. We put click to call buttons on every mobile page, prominent enquiry forms with spam protection and email alerts, and your phone number where a worried visitor can find it in a second. The easier it is to reach you, the more enquiries you get.

SEO ready foundations and schema

Your site is built so search engines can find, understand and rank it: a clean structure, proper titles and headings, indexable pages, clean URLs and schema markup for legal services. It becomes a foundation that ongoing SEO can build on, rather than a problem SEO has to fix first.

Accessibility for everyone

A good legal website is readable and usable by everyone, including people using screen readers or struggling with small text. We build with accessibility in mind, mindful of WCAG guidance, with sensible contrast, clear structure and proper labels, so no potential client is shut out.

Security and SSL

Clients trust you with sensitive information, so security is not optional. Every site ships with an SSL certificate and HTTPS as standard, secure hosting and regular backups, so enquiries are handled safely and your site stays online and trustworthy when it matters.

GDPR and cookie consent

An Irish law firm site has to handle personal data correctly. We build contact forms, cookie consent and data handling to meet GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018, so the enquiries your site collects are gathered and stored the right way from day one.

Content management you control

We build on a content management system, usually WordPress, so your team can update text, add a new solicitor or publish an article without writing a line of code. You stay in control of your own site, and we are here when you want a hand.

Built for Irish firms

A website that stays inside the LSRA rules and GDPR

Marketing a law firm in Ireland is not the same as marketing any other business, and your website is advertising. The Legal Services Regulatory Authority sets clear limits on how solicitors can advertise under the Legal Services Regulation Act 2015, with particular restrictions around personal injury work. Most generic web designers have never heard of those rules. Get it wrong and you risk more than a wasted budget.

We know those rules, and we build every page, testimonial and call to action to stay the right side of them: truthful, professional and never misleading, with proper disclaimers where they are needed. We also handle GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 properly, with compliant forms, cookie consent to Irish standards and secure handling of the enquiries your site collects.

Compliance areas we cover

  • LSRA advertising restrictions, including personal injury
  • Truthful, non misleading claims and outcome language
  • Proper disclaimers on solicitor websites
  • GDPR compliant contact forms and data handling
  • Cookie consent that meets Irish standards
  • Compliant display of reviews and testimonials
You focus on practising law. We build the website and keep it compliant, end to end.
